About Mamburao

Ang Bayan ng Mamburao

Mamburao is the capital municipality of Occidental Mindoro in MIMAROPA Region (Region IV-B). It is classified as a 2nd income class municipality, and is the most urbanized locality in the province.

The town is situated along the coast of Mamburao Bay and the banks of the Mamburao River, on the northwestern coast of Mindoro island. The municipality covers a total land area of 283.51 km², encompassing coastal plains, rolling terrain, and forested uplands.

Mamburao became the provincial capital of Occidental Mindoro on June 13, 1950, when the island of Mindoro was divided into Oriental and Occidental Mindoro.

According to the 2020 Philippine Statistics Authority census, Mamburao had a population of 44,522. The 2024 census figure of 44,554 is the most recently reported count. The municipality has a population density of approximately 157 persons per km². It is composed of 15 barangays, 8 of which form the poblacion (town center).

Brief History. Mamburao has one of the longest settlement histories on Mindoro island. During the Spanish colonial period, it was among the first localities visited in Mindoro. Today it serves as the political, economic, and administrative center of Occidental Mindoro, hosting key provincial government offices, commercial establishments, and health and educational facilities.
